Support

Something not working, or a file that won't open? Write to shidin@shidin.com. Please include your device, iOS version and the app version (Settings → About). If a file fails, tell us its format and size; only attach the file if you're happy to share it.

Common questions

Which formats can Peek3D open?

STL (binary and ASCII), 3MF (including painted multicolor files and plates from popular slicers), STEP/STP, IGES, OBJ with MTL and textures, PLY, glTF/GLB, USDZ/USD, FBX, DAE, 3DS, AMF, OFF, and ZIP files that contain any of these.

How do I open a file from another app?

In Files, Mail, Safari or AirDrop, tap the file, then Share → Peek3D. Inside Peek3D, tap “+” in the Library.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Peek3D → Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Peek3D Pro, or follow Apple's guide. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the anonymous statistics you can turn off.

The measurements look 1000× too big or small.

Some files don't say which unit they use. In the viewer, open Info and change the model's units; Peek3D remembers your choice for that file.
