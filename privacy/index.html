Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Peek3D (“the app”) is made by Shidin, an independent developer in Türkiye (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: shidin@shidin.com.

The short version

  • Your 3D files never leave your device. Opening, measuring, checking and converting all happen on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking across apps. We never ask for or use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) to help us fix problems and improve features. You can turn this off in Settings → Share anonymous usage data & crash reports.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to know whether you have Pro. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Your files

Models you open or import are read and stored only inside the app on your device (and in your iCloud or device backups if you use them). The Files-app previews and thumbnails made by Peek3D are also created on your device. When you export or share a file, it goes only where you send it. Snapshots are saved to your Photos library only when you ask; for that the app asks for add-only access and never reads your photos.

File names, folder names, file paths and the content of your models are never included in analytics, crash reports or purchase data.

2. What we collect and why

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

What: events such as “app opened”, “a file was opened” (with its format, a size range like “1–10 MB”, a triangle-count range and how long it took to read), which tool was used, which export format was chosen, that the upgrade screen was shown (and from where), a purchase or restore result (product and an error code if it failed), and whether an update notice was shown. Along with these, Google Analytics records a random app-instance ID created for this installation, the device model, operating system and app version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from the network connection. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

Why: to learn which formats and tools matter, find slow files and broken flows, and decide what to build next.

Advertising features are off: no advertising identifier is collected, consent for ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization is set to “denied”, Google signals are off, and analytics data is not shared with Google for advertising.

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

What: when the app crashes or hits a serious error, a report with the stack trace, app version, device model, operating system, free memory and disk space, a random Crashlytics installation ID, and the last few app events listed above (for example “opened an STL file of 10–50 MB”).

Why: to find and fix crashes. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

App configuration — Firebase Remote Config

What: the app regularly downloads a small set of settings from Firebase, for example the oldest app version that still works. To do this it sends a random Firebase installation ID, the app version, and your language, country and time zone.

Why: to tell you when an update is available or required. This check is needed for the app to work safely, so it is not affected by the sharing setting below.

Purchases — Apple and Adapty

What: when you start a trial, subscribe, buy Pro or restore a purchase, Apple processes the payment. Adapty, our subscription service, receives the transaction details from Apple and the app (product, price, currency, dates, transaction ID), a random Adapty profile ID, a device identifier for this installation (including the identifier for vendor, IDFV), device model, operating system, language, App Store country, and when the upgrade screen was shown. We turn off Adapty's IP-address and advertising-identifier collection.

Why: to unlock Pro on your devices, restore purchases, show correct prices, and understand which upgrade offers work.

While usage sharing is on, the app also gives Adapty the random Google Analytics app-instance ID, so that subscription events (such as a trial turning into a subscription) appear in our usage statistics.

3. Your choice: turning sharing off

In Settings → Share anonymous usage data & crash reports (on by default) you can stop usage statistics and crash reports at any time. When it is off, the app stops sending analytics events and crash reports from this device and stops passing the analytics ID to Adapty. Purchase handling and the update check keep working because the app needs them.

Deleting the app removes your files and resets all the random identifiers above; a reinstall starts with new ones.

4. What we don't do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or contact details.
  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites (so the app never shows Apple's tracking prompt).
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No selling or renting of data.

5. Legal bases (EU, UK, Türkiye)

Usage statistics and crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and improving it, balanced by the off switch. Purchases: performing our contract with you. Update checks: our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working.

7. Service providers and transfers

Google (Firebase, Google Analytics) and Adapty process data for us under their data-processing terms and may do so outside your country, for example in the United States or the European Union, with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Their policies: Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and security in Firebase, Adapty Privacy Policy. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its use, or complain to your data-protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, the data above isn't tied to your name or email; write to us and we'll help with what we can find. You can also simply turn sharing off or delete the app.

9. Children

Peek3D is a general tool for 3D files and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes
